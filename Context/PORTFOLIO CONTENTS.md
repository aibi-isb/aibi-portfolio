I enjoy working at the point where design and development meet. My projects often begin with understanding a problem, gathering requirements, organizing information, and mapping user flows. I then translate that work into interface designs, reusable components, database structures, and functional applications.

My experience has developed through academic team projects, independent product ideas, practical assignments, and self-directed learning. These experiences have allowed me to contribute as a product designer, systems analyst, frontend developer, database designer, researcher, and group leader.

I am particularly interested in junior UI and UX, frontend development, product design, and technology roles where I can continue learning while contributing across disciplines.

## My process

### Understand

I begin by identifying the users, the problem, the constraints, and the intended result. Depending on the project, this can involve stakeholder conversations, field research, assignment analysis, or independent product research.

### Structure

I organize the information into requirements, user roles, workflows, navigation, data models, and system specifications.

### Design

I explore layouts and interactions in Figma, establish visual hierarchy, and create reusable patterns that can work across different devices.

### Build

I translate the designs into functional interfaces using the technology that best fits the project.

### Validate and refine

I review responsive behaviour, interactions, data, and implementation quality. Feedback is described honestly as informal, structured, or planned depending on how it was collected.
