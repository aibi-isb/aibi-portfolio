 Enhance the scroll-driven GSAP laptop animation for the laptop section, optimized for a clear and intuitive user experience.

 As the laptop enters the viewport, it should smoothly rotate from its initial angled, slightly away-facing position until it is centered and facing the user. Once it reaches this position, keep the laptop stable while the user navigates through the five content states displayed directly inside the laptop screen:

 1. **01 — Understand**
 2. **02 — Structure**
 3. **03 — Design**
 4. **04 — Build**
 5. **05 — Validate**

 Scrolling should function as deliberate step-based navigation:

 - Scrolling down advances to the next content item.
 - Scrolling up returns to the previous content item.
 - Each scroll gesture should transition between one content state at a time rather than continuously skipping through multiple items.
 - The active content should transition smoothly inside the laptop screen using a refined fade, slide, or crossfade animation.
 - The laptop itself should remain centered, front-facing, and visually stable while the five screen states change.

 Use GSAP and `ScrollTrigger` to create a pinned, scroll-controlled section. Add scroll snapping or equivalent controlled progression so each item settles cleanly into place. Prevent accidental rapid scrolling from skipping multiple stages, while still keeping the interaction responsive and natural.

 After **05 — Validate** is displayed and the user scrolls beyond the final stage, the laptop should remain open and gradually rotate around its vertical axis into an angled, slightly away-facing position. It should return to the same orientation it had before entering the section, without folding the screen or closing the laptop.

The rotation should be smooth, restrained, and consistent with the laptop’s initial entrance movement. Preserve the laptop’s open angle, proportions, perspective, lighting, shadows, and scale throughout the transition. Do not close the screen, fold the laptop, or introduce any abrupt changes in position. The animation should work naturally in both directions when the user scrolls up or down.

Maintain consistent perspective, lighting, shadows, scale, and screen framing throughout the animation. The content must remain properly contained within the laptop display, with no overflow, clipping, or misalignment. Avoid automatic playback, abrupt jumps, uncontrolled scrolling, or transitions that occur independently of user input.

Enhance the UX of the scroll gesture to produce a subtle, coordinated movement across the entire section—not just inside the laptop screen.

Only deal with the laptop. The Tab and the phone for smaller viewports will be addressed later.
