## Audience and message — E

Small businesses and freelance clients need a clear offer and relevant proof. Recruiters and engineering teams need a realistic level, contribution details, technical judgment and maintainable work. Present Ibrahim as a junior practitioner with practical project experience. Academic work is credible evidence when its context is explicit.

## Editorial rules — E principles, P checklist

Write plainly: what was built, for whom or for what assignment, how it works and what Ibrahim contributed. Prefer “implemented the booking form” to broad claims such as “revolutionized rental management.” Clearly distinguish research, prototypes, working features and future plans.

Use “Academic project” and “Personal project” only when confirmed. “Client project” requires evidence of a real client engagement. State team and AI assistance where material. Quantified outcomes require the measurement method, conditions and source; otherwise describe demonstrated functionality and limitations.

No generic hype, unsupported testimonials, placeholder client logos or inflated seniority. Keep project titles consistent across the portfolio, GitHub, CV and LinkedIn. A CV should point to the verified GitHub profile; case studies link to relevant repositories. Reuse approved case-study facts across freelance profiles, without turning academic work into paid experience.

## Contact content — P/V

Request a brief description of the goal, current situation and desired timing. Budget may be optional if a form is added. Use only verified email and social links. A résumé link is optional and appears only when the actual reviewed file exists. Do not invent contact information.
