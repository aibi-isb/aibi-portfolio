## Author checklist — remove before publication

- Origin, maturity and role are explicit and accurate.
- Team/AI assistance is described proportionately.
- Goals are separate from verified outcomes; roadmap is separate from shipped features.
- Images are real, labeled and cleared for publication; sensitive data is removed.
- Claims align with the repository, README and demo.
- Links work; missing links and bracketed prompts are removed.
- Headings, captions, alt text and narrow-screen layout are checked.
